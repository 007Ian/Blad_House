Illustrator & Publishing Consultant

Jessica Cleaver Blad

Jessica Cleaver Blad is an illustrator, designer, and storyteller with a passion for bringing children’s stories to life. She earned her degree in Illustration & Publishing from Brigham Young University–Idaho and has illustrated and published multiple children’s books, including her most recent title, If I Could Fly. Jessica’s mission is to help aspiring authors share their stories with the world through thoughtful, imaginative illustrations. She enjoys guiding new authors through the self-publishing process, making the journey approachable. She loves the moment when the book is finally held by the author.
